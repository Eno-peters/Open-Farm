# 🌱 OpenFarms

> Offline-first AI-powered farm planning, crop monitoring, and post-harvest decision platform for smallholder farmers.

OpenFarms is an AI-powered agricultural platform designed to help smallholder farmers make informed decisions throughout the farming lifecycle—from planning and planting to crop monitoring, harvesting, and post-harvest storage.

Built for low-connectivity environments, OpenFarms combines computer vision, weather intelligence, explainable AI, and structured agricultural knowledge to provide practical recommendations that farmers can trust. Instead of treating planting, disease diagnosis, and harvesting as separate tasks, the platform connects them into one continuous farming journey, ensuring every decision is informed by previous farm records and observations.



## 🚜 The Problem

## Across Sub-Saharan Africa, up to 30–50% of harvested food never reaches a buyer, resulting in an estimated **$4 billion in annual losses**. Smallholder farmers often lack access to timely, localized decision support, while existing digital tools provide generic recommendations that ignore factors such as crop stage, weather, soil conditions, farm history, and post-harvest management.

 💡 Our Solution

OpenFarms delivers an offline-capable AI platform that helps farmers make better decisions throughout the farming lifecycle.

The platform enables farmers to:

* 🌱 Plan planting seasons
* 📷 Diagnose crop health using AI
* 🌦 Receive weather-aware recommendations
* 🌾 Track harvests and storage conditions
* 📊 Maintain connected farm records
* 🤖 Receive explainable AI recommendations

Every recommendation is supported by contextual farm data, weather information, AI models, and agricultural knowledge rather than isolated predictions.

## 🤖 How AI Works

OpenFarms uses a hybrid AI approach that combines multiple intelligence layers:

* **MobileNetV3-Small** for tomato and maize leaf image classification
* **Rule-based recommendation engine** for planting, harvesting, and storage decisions
* **Structured agricultural knowledge layer** for converting technical outputs into farmer-friendly guidance
* **Weather and crop-cycle data integration** to improve recommendation accuracy

This approach produces recommendations that are transparent, practical, and explainable rather than acting as a black-box prediction engine.
